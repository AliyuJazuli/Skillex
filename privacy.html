Privacy Policy

Skillex is a skill-sharing and learning platform. This policy explains what information is handled when you browse the site, request an account, contact the team, or use an account.

Information you provide

An account access request includes your name and email address. Contact messages include your name, reply email, and message. Account holders may provide a display name, email, biography, learning and teaching goals, profile photo, saved skills, lesson notes, quiz results, progress, and exchange requests.

How information is used

Skillex uses this information to review account requests, respond to messages, operate user accounts, show peer skill matches, save learning progress, and maintain app security. Profile names, biographies, and skill interests may be visible to signed-in members so they can discover peers.

Services that process information

Account, profile, learning, and exchange data are stored in the dedicated Skillex Supabase project. Profile photos are stored in a private bucket and served through time-limited links. Access requests are stored in a private Supabase table and reviewed by the Skillex owner. Contact messages are sent to the configured recipient through Gmail and are not stored in the owner dashboard. Community posts are shared with members and may be moderated by the owner. Email correspondence is also handled by the email services used by the sender and recipient.

Cookies and local storage

The app uses browser local storage for appearance preferences and as a cache for learning data. Authentication sessions are managed by Supabase. Clearing browser storage can remove local preferences; signed-in learning data is synchronized with the account when the app can reach Supabase.

Data choices and requests

Signed-in members can export and import learning data or permanently delete their account from Settings. Account deletion removes the profile, profile photo, access request information, and associated learning, exchange, and community data. To ask a question or request correction of information, use the contact form. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

Children and account access

Skillex is intended for people who can use an online learning service under the laws that apply to them. New accounts are currently created by the Skillex team after an access request; submitting a request does not automatically create an account.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as Skillex changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means the revised policy is available for review.

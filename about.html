Our story

We’re building a more generous way to learn.

Skillex was created for students who want growth without barriers, where knowledge becomes a shared resource instead of a paid service.

Our story

We noticed that many students had valuable skills but little access to affordable learning. Skillex turns that gap into an opportunity by connecting people who want to teach with people who want to learn.

Mission

Make peer-to-peer learning accessible, practical, and empowering for every student.

Vision

Create a campus culture where sharing knowledge feels natural, rewarding, and community-driven.

Why choose Skillex

Built for students, designed for momentum.

Free to join

No subscriptions. No hidden fees. Just practical learning and generous exchange.

Flexible exchange

Learn by teaching, or teach by learning — both are welcome.

Supportive community

Every session is designed to feel encouraging, modern, and student-first.

About the product

Built to exchange skills, share knowledge, and grow together.

A HYDEV product

Skillex is a HYDEV product created by Aliyu Jazuli, connecting people who want to teach with people who want to learn.

Technology stack

Skillex uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Supabase Auth, Postgres, Row Level Security, and private avatar storage. The site is hosted on Netlify.
